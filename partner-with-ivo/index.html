Clear expectations

Good partnerships start with clear expectations.

Customers, contractors and vendors bring different responsibilities to a commercial assignment. IVO’s partner approach gives each party a relevant entry point while keeping the scope, the business relationship and the information shared with others understandable.

01

Customers define the requirement

Explain the property objective, available information and the way your organization authorizes work. Your existing contractors or internal teams may remain part of the arrangement. The conversation should identify where IVO is being asked to contribute rather than assume an entire property program is changing hands.

02

Contractors explain their capability

A useful application describes the services your business actually performs, the locations it can support and its applicable qualifications. Be specific about availability and limitations. Reviewed opportunity access allows eligible providers to consider relevant requests, not a guarantee of assignments or volume.

03

Vendors clarify supply & service

Supplier relationships depend on reliable ordering and remittance information as well as product knowledge. Identify the correct contacts, the categories you supply and the responsibilities you retain around delivery, returns or warranty questions. Purchase terms belong in the applicable agreement or order.

Illustrative requirement

Several independent businesses, one defined requirement.

An illustrative arrangement: a customer has a defined commercial scope, a contractor contributes the relevant trade capability and a vendor provides specified materials. IVO’s workspace can keep the shared project discussion organized while each party’s sensitive onboarding paperwork remains in its restricted area.

  1. Use the customer, contractor or vendor entry path that fits your role.
  2. Complete the relevant business review and share only appropriate documents.
  3. Confirm the applicable scope, responsibilities and authorizations for each assignment.

For customers & property teams

Bring a project, repair or defined commercial need. Share the location, desired result, scope information and timing so the team can review the appropriate service path.

IVO can be an additional resource alongside your established contractors, internal teams and preferred vendors. A relationship may begin with a single location, an individual trade requirement or a project that needs a different combination of resources. Explain where your team needs support and which responsibilities are already covered.

Customer organizations have different onboarding and purchasing processes. Identify the authorized contact, any vendor requirements and the way work is approved. That allows the conversation to address how the relationship would operate as well as the physical scope being considered.

For contractors & vendors

Independent businesses can submit their capabilities, commercial experience, service coverage and availability for IVO’s partner review.

Contractors, specialty trades, service providers and suppliers contribute different capabilities. Describe the work your own business performs, the areas it covers, its typical availability and any limitations on the scopes it can accept. A precise capability profile is more useful than selecting every service category.

Vendors can also explain ordering contacts, lead-time considerations and the products or materials they supply. Contractor and vendor applications are reviewed as business profiles; neither submission creates employment, exclusivity, a purchase order or a promise of future assignments.

Customer requests

Use Request Service for a property need, or submit plans and procurement details through a bid opportunity. Supporting documents can be added through the portal.

Provide enough context for the team to understand the objective and the procurement stage. If you are seeking an early discussion, say so; if you have a defined scope or issued bid package, identify the current documents and requested response date. Your portal keeps later questions and attachments connected to that submission.

Provider applications

Provide company and contact information, trades, service areas, availability, applicable qualifications and a commercial reference.

The initial application introduces your business and its capabilities. Use Secure Onboarding for requested tax forms, remittance details and other sensitive paperwork, rather than placing them in a shared project file. Keep the authorized contact current so IVO can request clarification or updated qualifications from the correct person.

01Eligibility review

Application submission does not guarantee approval or work. Provider opportunity access is reviewed by state, trade and review expiry; project-specific release checks remain separate.

Opportunity access is separate from merely holding a portal account. The team reviews the relevant service areas, trades and qualification information before enabling provider access. A specific project can require further review of scope, availability and credentials even when a business already participates in the network.

02Working on an assigned project

Use the shared workspace for scope questions, updates, documents and closeout. Confirm the contracting arrangement and required authorizations before starting work.

An assigned workspace gives the customer, participating provider and IVO a place to organize relevant updates and files. Confirm who is responsible for materials, site access, approvals and completion records. If the requirement changes, raise the question through the project and obtain the applicable decision before treating the change as authorized.

A few practical answers

Before the next step.

Still have a question? Call (206) 539-9549 or email office@ivoservgroup.com.

Should I apply as a contractor or a vendor?

Use contractor for a business offering the relevant trade or field-service capability, and vendor for a supplier or other business whose relationship is primarily around products or supply. If your company does both, explain the activities clearly so the team can review the appropriate arrangement.

Where do tax forms and payment instructions belong?

Use Secure Onboarding for requested sensitive paperwork. Ordinary request attachments and shared project documents are intended for the scope and its coordination. Payment setup information does not initiate a debit, transfer or card charge.

What happens after we submit an application?

The IVO team reviews the business information and may request clarification or additional documents. Provider opportunity access and project participation require the applicable separate reviews. Keep your profile and authorized contact current while the application is being considered.
