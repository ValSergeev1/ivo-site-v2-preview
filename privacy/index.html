Privacy notice · Last updated November 17, 2026

How IVO Group handlesyour information.

This notice covers ivoservgroup.com and the IVO customer, technician and staff apps run by IVO Serv Group (“IVO Group”, “we”).

What we collect

  • What you send us. Requests and partner applications: name, email, phone, company, property location, a description of the work, and for contractors and vendors, trades, service areas and business details.
  • Account information. For people we invite to an IVO app: name, email, phone, role, language, and sign-in and security records (for example the time and device type of a sign-in). Staff accounts also use an authenticator app for two-step sign-in.
  • Work records. Scopes, estimates, approvals, messages, photos, documents, voice notes, invoices and payment status for the work you are part of.
  • Technician check-ins. When a technician checks in or out of a job in the app, the location and its accuracy at that moment. We do not track location in the background.
  • Contractor compliance documents. Insurance, license and tax form details that contractors submit, with expiry dates.
  • Text messages and push alerts. Your phone number and consent, only if you turn text alerts on. Reply STOP at any time.
  • Technical data. Server logs (shortened IP address, browser type, pages requested, errors) used to keep the service secure and working. The website uses no advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To review and respond to requests and applications, and to scope, schedule, run, document and bill the work you approve.
  • To match work with contractors by trade, service area and the requirement.
  • To send service email (sign-in codes, receipts, job updates) and, where allowed, updates about IVO services. Every update or marketing email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To keep the platform secure, prevent fraud and keep required business records.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

AI features

The IVO apps include an AI assistant and document reading (for example reading a scope from a plan or photo). These run on Google Cloud (Gemini on Vertex AI) under IVO's Google Cloud terms. The assistant only sees what the signed-in person can already see, and it asks before taking any action.

Who we share it with

  • People on your work. The customer, the assigned technicians or contractors, and IVO staff see the parts of a job they need.
  • Service providers that run parts of the platform for us under contract: Google (hosting, database, email and AI), our email delivery provider, and, once turned on, our payment processor, accounting software (QuickBooks) and text message provider. Card and bank numbers are entered only on the payment processor's page; IVO does not store them.
  • When required by law, or to protect people, property or the platform.

Where it's kept and for how long

IVO's platform is hosted on Google Cloud in the United States, with encrypted backups. We keep work and billing records for as long as needed for the work, warranties, taxes and legal requirements, and delete or anonymize other information when it is no longer needed.

Your choices and rights

  • Ask to see, correct or delete your information, or ask how it is used, by emailing office@ivoservgroup.com. Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have additional rights; we will not treat you differently for using them.
  • Unsubscribe from update and marketing email with the link in any of those emails (how it works).
  • Turn text alerts off in your app settings or reply STOP.

Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 16.

Changes and contact
