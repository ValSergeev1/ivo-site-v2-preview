Parties. IVO Serv Group ("IVO") and the independent contractor or vendor business ("Contractor") that accepts work through the IVO apps. Contractor is an independent business, not an employee or agent of IVO.

1. What these terms cover

Fees, payment timing, retainage, lien waivers, deductions and disputes for work IVO awards to Contractor (job offers, dispatches, awarded bids and sub requests). Scope, schedule and price for each job are in that job's award.

2. Joining the network

  • Applying and staying listed: [$0 / OWNER TO SET].
  • Required documents before work is routed: general liability, workers' compensation (where required), contractor license (where required), auto liability (when vehicles are used on site), W-9. Minimum limits: [OWNER TO SET PER TRADE]. Expired documents pause new work until renewed.

3. IVO's fee

  • Option A — margin model. IVO prices the job to the customer; Contractor is paid the awarded amount in full. No fee is deducted from Contractor's pay.
  • Option B — coordination fee. [X]% of each approved pay request, deducted from the payout, capped at [$ CAP] per job.
  • Option C — flat fee. [$ AMOUNT] per completed job / per month.

Emergency dispatch premium (if any) paid to Contractor: [OWNER TO SET].

4. Getting paid

  • Contractor submits a pay request in the IVO app with photos and closeout items. Progress payments allowed on jobs over [$ THRESHOLD].
  • Retainage: [10]% of each progress payment is held until the customer signs off (matches the platform default in Settings → Payments; owner may change it).
  • A lien waiver (conditional or unconditional, progress or final) is required for each payment before it is released.
  • Payment timing: within [N] days after IVO approves the pay request [or: after IVO is paid by the customer — OWNER TO DECIDE; check state prompt-payment rules for WA, OR, ID and CA].
  • Method: payouts through IVO's payment processor to Contractor's verified bank account. Processor fees: [paid by IVO / passed through at cost — OWNER TO SET]. IVO never stores bank or card numbers.

5. Deductions and back-charges

Only for: work not done to the awarded scope and not corrected after notice [N] days; damage caused by Contractor; amounts agreed in writing. Each back-charge is itemized in the app with photos, and Contractor can respond before it is applied.

6. Changes to scope

Extra work needs an approved change order in the app before it is done. Unapproved extra work is not paid.

7. Refunds and chargebacks

If a customer payment is refunded or charged back because of Contractor's work, IVO may recover the matching amount from future payouts after the dispute process in section 8. [OWNER TO CONFIRM]

8. Disputes

Raise it in the job's messages or at office@ivoservgroup.com within [N] days. IVO responds within [N] business days. Unresolved disputes: [mediation in COUNTY, STATE / OWNER TO SET].

9. Taxes and insurance

Contractor is responsible for its own taxes, licenses, permits (unless the award says IVO pulls them), insurance and its workers. IVO issues 1099s where required.

10. Changes to these terms

IVO gives [30] days' notice in the app and by email before fee changes apply to new work. Work already awarded keeps the terms in effect at award.
