Commercial Construction & Property Services in Kent and Puget Sound

IVO Group is based in Kent, Washington, and works with property managers, facilities managers, commercial landlords, HOAs, and retail and restaurant teams across Puget Sound. One IVO contact scopes the work, coordinates the technicians and trade contractors involved, and keeps a written record through closeout.

Services

What IVO Group handles in Puget Sound

Single-contact coordination across commercial construction, ongoing property maintenance, turnovers, and urgent damage response.

01 / Build

Commercial Construction & Tenant Improvements

Tenant improvements, interior renovations, build-outs, and defined trade packages for offices, retail, industrial, and restaurant spaces.
02 / Maintain

Property Services & Turnovers

Suite turnovers, make-ready work, recurring commercial repairs, and multi-trade maintenance coordinated through one contact.

Specialty & Multi-Trade Coordination

Scheduled multi-trade projects, compliance repairs, and property upgrades where sequencing and site access matter.

Accountability

Why work with one contact

Clear scope and written records from initial walkthrough through closeout.

01

Written scope before work starts

Written scope and price—or an agreed not-to-exceed limit—before crews mobilize, with inclusions, exclusions, and assumptions stated clearly.

02

Photos and closeout kept together

Photos, field notes, and closeout records stay attached to the request so property teams have one clean record. See how to work with IVO.

03

Changes approved before work proceeds

Scope changes are documented and approved in writing before additional work is performed.
