How it works

One contact,
a written record.

Property teams get the same steps on every request, so it is clear where the work stands and who has the next action.

  1. Share the need

    Send the property address, the condition, photos and the timing you need.

  2. Written scope & price

    You get a written scope and price, or a not-to-exceed limit, before work starts.

  3. Changes approved first

    If something changes, you approve the change and its cost before that work is done.

  4. Closeout on record

    Photos, notes and closeout documents stay with the request.

What should I send with a request?

The property address, what is happening and where, the result you want, and any timing or access limits. Photos help the team understand the condition before the first conversation.

What if it is an emergency?

Call (206) 539-9549. A submitted form does not dispatch a crew. You can send photos and details through the form after the call.

Do you work with multi-site portfolios?

Yes. Send the addresses and the way your organization issues work orders. Each location and scope is kept separate so site contacts, approvals and records are not mixed up between properties.

Is my property in your area?

Our office is in Kent, Washington. For an Idaho property, send the address and scope and IVO confirms coverage for that property before work is accepted.
