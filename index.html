01 / Build

Commercial construction

A tenant move-in, a refreshed customer area and a single trade package need different information and sequencing, even in the same building. We start from how the space has to work when it's finished, then plan backward from your opening or lease date.

Typical scope

  • Tenant improvements & second-generation suites
  • Office, retail & restaurant interiors
  • Demising walls, framing & drywall
  • Ceilings, flooring, paint & finishes
  • Doors, frames, hardware & millwork
  • Accessibility & code-driven updates

What you receive

  • Defined scope with inclusions & exclusions
  • Line-item budget before work starts
  • Schedule built around occupancy & access
  • Weekly photo progress in your portal
  • Change requests approved before work
  • Closeout package: photos, punch list, documents

02 / Maintain

Property services

  • Suite turnovers between tenants
  • Storefronts, doors, locks & closers
  • Drywall, paint, ceiling tile & flooring repair

  • Recurring or portfolio-wide support

What you receive

03 / Respond

Emergency property response

Unexpected damage creates several decisions at once: is the area safe, what temporary protection is needed, and what will the permanent repair be? We separate the urgent condition from the permanent fix so nothing is decided in a rush.

Typical scope

  • Board-up & temporary securing
  • Vehicle or impact damage
  • Leak containment & protection
  • Make-safe of damaged areas
  • Follow-up permanent repair

What you receive

  • Written repair plan within 24 hours
  • Documentation your insurer will ask for
  • One record from first call to final repair

FAQ

Before the
next step.

Still have a question? Call (206) 539-9549 or email office@ivoservgroup.com.

Is IVO a general contractor or a referral service?

Both, depending on what you need. IVO can consult on scope, act as the general contractor that runs the project, or coordinate independent trades from our network. You always have one IVO contact, and responsibilities for each assignment are written down before work starts.

What size projects do you take on?

Everything from a single door repair to a multi-trade tenant improvement or a GC trade package. Each request is reviewed so we can confirm the right path, team and timeline before you commit.

How fast do you respond?

It depends on priority. Emergency calls are answered by a person, and we target a crew on site within 4 hours in served metro areas. Urgent requests get a reply within 2 business hours, and standard requests the same business day. See the response commitments above for the full breakdown.

How do I know what it will cost?

You get a written scope and price, or a not-to-exceed limit, before work starts. If something changes, you get a change request with the cost to approve before that work happens. Invoices are itemized against the approved scope.

Can I track my request?

Yes. After IVO reviews your request, the authorized contact is invited to your own project workspace with status, photos, documents, approvals and messages. Each team member gets an individual invited login.

How are contractors chosen?

Network contractors apply, submit their business documents and are matched to work by trade, service area, capacity and the requirement itself. IVO stays responsible for coordination and communication with you.

Do you work with brokers, realtors and owners preparing a space?

Yes. We scope and complete make-ready, pre-listing and lease-up work, and give you clear pricing you can use in conversations with buyers or tenants.

Where do you work?

Washington, Oregon, Idaho and Northern California. For other locations or multi-site portfolios, send the addresses and we'll confirm coverage.
