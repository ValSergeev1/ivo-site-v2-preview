What IVO can organize

Scope and deliverables.

Typical scope

  • General repairs and work orders
  • Suite turnovers between tenants
  • Storefronts, doors, locks and closers
  • Drywall, paint, ceiling tile and flooring repair
  • Fixture and restroom repairs
  • Recurring or portfolio-wide support

What you receive

  • Response by priority tier
  • Not-to-exceed approval before extra cost
  • Before and after photos on every job
  • Itemized invoice tied to the work order
  • Portfolio view across locations
  • Repeat-issue tracking by property

Process

You bring the requirement. We help define the path.

  1. Share the need

    Location, condition, the result you want and timing.

  2. Review & define the scope

    IVO confirms the path and agrees price or a not-to-exceed.

  3. Coordinate the work

    Vetted trades matched by skill, area and capacity.

  4. Document completion

    Photos, approvals and closeout files in your record.

Illustrative requirement

A turnover creates a list of smaller requirements.

We walk the suite, group the punch items by trade, price them against a not-to-exceed and run them in the order that gets the space leasable fastest.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do you offer recurring maintenance?

Yes. Recurring or portfolio-wide service can be set up with agreed response tiers and a single invoice format across properties.

What if the repair costs more than expected?

Work stops at the approved not-to-exceed. You get a change request with the cost to approve before anything extra happens.
