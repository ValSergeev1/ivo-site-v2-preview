Process

From first call to closeout.

  1. Share the need

    Tell us the property, the condition and the result you want. Photos and plans help.

  2. Review & scope

    IVO confirms the path, writes the scope and agrees price or a not-to-exceed before work starts.

  3. Coordinate the work

    Vetted trades are matched by skill, location and capacity. One IVO lead runs it.

  4. Document & close

    Before, during and after photos, approvals and closeout files saved to your record.
