Process

A clear path from request to closeout.

Consultant, general contractor or coordinated network: you always have one IVO contact, and responsibilities for each assignment are written down before work starts.

Process

From the first call to the final sign-off.

  1. Start with the property

    Share the location, condition, objective and timing.

  2. Clarify scope & compare

    IVO defines the scope and, where it helps, compares partner bids.

  3. Confirm the arrangement

    Price or not-to-exceed, schedule and responsibilities agreed in writing.

  4. Track progress & close out

    Status, photos, approvals and closeout files in your workspace.

Delivery models

Consultant. General contractor. Network.

Consultant

Help defining a scope, comparing options or preparing a requirement before anyone prices it.

General contractor

IVO runs the project and is responsible for coordinating the trades and the schedule.

Contractor network

Vetted independent trades matched to the work, with IVO coordinating communication with you.

The IVO app

Private access starts with an invitation.

After IVO reviews a request, each authorized person gets an individual login at app.ivoservgroup.com. Customers see their own properties and jobs; partners see only the work assigned to them.

  • Individual accounts, no shared logins
  • Approvals and change requests in one tap
  • Photos, documents and messages on the job record
  • Works on a phone at the property
