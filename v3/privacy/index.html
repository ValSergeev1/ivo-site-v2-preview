Draft · pending review

What we collect

When you send a request or a partner application we receive the details you type: name, email, phone, company, property location, the description of the work and, for partners, trades, service areas and capabilities.

How we use it

Only to review and respond to your request or application, to run the work you approve, and to keep the project record. We don't sell personal information and the site has no advertising trackers.

Where it's kept

In IVO's own platform, hosted on Google Cloud in the United States. Access is limited to IVO staff who need it and, for assigned work, the people on that job.

Your choices

Ask us to see, correct or delete your information at office@ivoservgroup.com.
