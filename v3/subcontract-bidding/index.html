Process

How a bid moves through IVO.

  1. Package received

    Invitations arrive by email, bid platform or this site and become a tracked package.

  2. Scope by trade

    Plans, specs, addenda and site photos are read and split by trade, with the source page noted.

  3. Partner pricing

    Vetted partners confirm price, dates and capacity from the IVO contractor app.

  4. Review & submit

    IVO levels quotes, sets the price and submits after internal approval.

What you get

A bid you can carry.

Clear inclusions

Every bid lists inclusions, exclusions and assumptions by trade, tied to the documents it was priced from.

On deadline

Deadlines are tracked from the moment a package lands. Late addenda are flagged and re-priced.

One accountable contact

A single IVO estimator answers scope questions before and after award.

FAQ

Bidding questions.

Can you bid on public or private work?

IVO reviews private and public invitations. Send the package and deadline and we confirm whether we're bidding within one business day (draft target).

Which trades do you price?

Defined trade packages across finishes, drywall, doors and hardware, repairs and multi-trade scopes. Ask about anything else; we'll say if it isn't a fit.

How do I send documents?

Use the request form with "Invite IVO to bid", or email bid@ivoservgroup.com. Platform invitations are accepted too.
