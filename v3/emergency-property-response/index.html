Process

Property emergency? Call now.

  1. Make the area safe

    Keep people clear of glass, water and damaged structure. Call 911 for fire, injury or gas.

  2. Call IVO

    Tell us the address, what happened and what's exposed. We confirm a crew and an arrival estimate.

  3. Temporary fix, then the permanent plan

    Board-up or protection now, photos for your insurer, and a written repair plan.

  4. Document & close

    Before, during and after photos, approvals and closeout files saved to your record.
